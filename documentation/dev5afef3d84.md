## product name

The *product name* is the name of your app as it will appear to
customers in the App Store and appear on the device when the app is
installed. The product name can't be shorter than 2 characters and can't
be longer than 255 bytes. The product name should be similar to the app
name that you enter later in App Store Connect.

For guidelines on choosing an app name, go to [App
information](https://help.apple.com/itunes-connect/developer/#/dev219b53a88){.URL}
in [App Store Connect
Help](https://help.apple.com/itunes-connect/developer/){.URL}.
